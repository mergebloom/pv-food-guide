Slow-cooked beef and goat birria, served in soft or crisp tacos with rich consommé. A neighborhood morning stop, rather than a late-night taquería.

YOUR FIRST ORDER

Ask for tacos dorados (crisp) or suaves (soft), with consommé on the side. Confirm which meat is available.

A FEW USEFUL WORDS

Order with a little confidence.

Burrita
A flour-tortilla wrap; here, seafood is the point.

Consommé
The savory birria broth. Ask for a cup alongside.

Con todo
Usually onion and cilantro on tacos. Confirm locally.

Bring small-denomination pesos as a precaution; payment methods and current prices are not verified. Ask about allergens and preparation — this guide does not certify dietary suitability.
