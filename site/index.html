A FEW USEFUL WORDS

Order with a little confidence.

Burrita
A flour-tortilla wrap; on the coast, often seafood.

Consommé
The savory birria broth. Ask for a cup alongside.

Al vapor
Steamed. At El Chulo, think tender beef-head tacos.

Bring small-denomination pesos as a precaution; current prices and payment methods are not independently verified. Ask about allergens and preparation. Historical article dishes are ordering ideas, not a guaranteed current menu.
